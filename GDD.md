# THE PLUG — GDD v0.1

## Fantasy
Start with $150, a half-ounce, three low-quality seeds, and an old shed behind Grandma's house. Build a personal underground empire through time management, business choices, people, properties, reputation, and consequences.

## Design pillars
- Bingeable, short sessions.
- Vertical mobile-first presentation.
- Stylized 3D world with simple readable graphics.
- Deep economy underneath a simple interface.
- No required ads.
- Premium Gold can accelerate or customize, but the full progression path remains earnable for free.
- No permanent character death.
- Consequences appear as events in the world rather than a visible heat meter.

## First neighborhood
Grandma's House, Your Shed, Garden Center, Gas Station, Liquor Store, Park, Police Station, Fire Department, a few houses and streets.

## Core currencies
- Cash: earned through gameplay and spent on progression.
- Gold: future premium currency.

## Core resources
Product inventory, seeds, growing supplies, properties, vehicles, employees, reputation.

## Core systems planned
Growing, idle progression, customer offers, travel time, employees, inventory, market conditions, reputation, attention/police events, robbery events, jail, property upgrades, vehicles, character customization, prestige/retirement, leaderboards.

## Prototype scope
The first vertical slice deliberately focuses on the beginning of the loop: acquire supplies → plant → wait → harvest → improve the operation.
